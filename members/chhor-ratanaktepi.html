Outlook

Vision & Collaboration

A background in ICT Policy combined with a current focus on Big Data and AI lets Ratanaktepi weigh both the technical and the societal side of AI development, so that research outcomes are technically sound as well as practically implementable. Through the MPTC-CBNU partnership she acts as a bridge between the two institutions, building human resources in digital skills and supporting knowledge transfer between Korea and Cambodia.

  • Advance AI development in Cambodia through applied research
  • Connect complex AI technologies to practical, everyday applications
  • Make emerging technologies accessible to broader audiences
