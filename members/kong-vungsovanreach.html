About

About

Applied AI researcher specializing in computer vision, with 7+ years in software development and 5+ years in ML, AI, and computer vision. He completed his PhD in Big Data at Chungbuk National University and served as a Postdoctoral Researcher at AICLab from March to August 2026, where he worked on LLM applications such as NL2SQL and agentic AI alongside computer vision, and now continues at AICLab as a Researcher. His research focuses on real-world AI for safety, healthcare, and surveillance, including medical image analysis and video-based monitoring.
